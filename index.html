Free Firebase Setup (3 Steps)

Takes only 2 minutes — no credit card required!

Step 1: Create Free Project

Open console.firebase.google.com with your Google account. Click "Add project" and enter name: green-trends-kothapet. Click Continue until created.

Step 2: Create Firestore Database

In your Firebase dashboard, click "Build" $\rightarrow$ "Firestore Database" $\rightarrow$ click "Create database". Select "Start in test mode" (allows free read/write) and click Enable.

Step 3: Register Web App & Copy Code

Click Project Settings (Gear icon ⚙️) $\rightarrow$ scroll down to "Your apps" $\rightarrow$ click the Web icon ``. Give it any nickname and click "Register app". Copy the generated const firebaseConfig = { ... } snippet and paste it into the Cloud Sync box!
